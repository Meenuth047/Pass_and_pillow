🎵 Pass the Pillow

Play music that stops randomly!

How to play:

1. Choose music: Connect Spotify playlist, upload local file, or use presets.

2. Select when the music should stop.

3. Pass the pillow until music stops automatically!

Spotify Requirements: Playback control requires a Spotify Premium account. The in-browser Web Player runs on desktop Chrome, Firefox, Edge, and Safari. For mobile or external devices, open the official Spotify app to play via Spotify Connect.
